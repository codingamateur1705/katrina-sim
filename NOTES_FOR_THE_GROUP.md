### Other game mechanics (all game values)
- **Strength** (Strong, OK, Tired, Exhausted, Very weak, Collapsing). Everyone starts Strong. It drops by 1 each round in an attic, on a roof, in a neighbour's house, upstairs, in the Superdome or Convention Center, each round for the Mayor, when food runs out, and for wading, swimming, walking or pushing a wheelchair. Locks: Kimberly's "Wade to the highway overpass" needs Tired or better, "Swim to a two-storey house" needs Exhausted or better, Herbert's "Push her toward the bridge" needs Exhausted or better.
- **Hours of waiting** on the fate screen are game estimates attached to choices (e.g. 6 hours in the Superdome line).
- **Distances** on the fate screen: Houston about 560 km, Baton Rouge about 130 km (road distance, rounded).
- **New locked option (game detail):** Lakeview round 2 at home, "Drive out now", locked: "Too late. The storm has closed the roads and the water is rising."
- **Mayor ending:** adds one line about the cost of the round 1 decision, taken from the round 1 consequences.

## 2. Changes to the starter content

- Kimberly, "Buy food and water, stay with Scott": removed "and start filming your street on a 20 dollar camcorder". It revealed the real story during the game. The camcorder is still in "What really happened".
- Lakeview, "Wait for an official order": now "When the order comes, the roads out are jammed. You decide to stay one more night. You still have your car." The old text ("you still have your car") did not explain why the family was still at home in round 2.
- Lakeview, round 3: "You see your street on TV" moved to the round 4 card, as the prompt suggested.
- Herbert, "Call 911": added "As the water rises, you carry Ethel upstairs." The choice moved him upstairs without saying so.
- Herbert, round 2 Superdome and round 3: the per-choice "Ethel worse" steps were partly replaced by the pump and fever cards, so Ethel does not drop twice for the same thing. Only the real path (waiting for the buses at the Convention Center) ends with Ethel's death.
- "{money}" placeholders replace fixed amounts like "$70 is left" and "$15", so the text stays right if prices change.
- Ending lines about money now use three levels: under $150 "not enough for a motel, food or a bus home", under $1,000 "It will not last long", else "savings and credit card cover a place to stay".
- Breaking news ticker never names a decision of the Mayor, so the Mayor group does not learn the real choice during the game.
